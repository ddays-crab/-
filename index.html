[Paragraph 1] Many people want to connect with others but find interacting in public hard. Social media offers a comfortable alternative. It lets users be part of the group without much risk. WePlay mixes games and social chat. Users create colorful online characters instead of showing their real faces. This extra bit of privacy can help shy users feel more relaxed.
【翻譯】許多人想與他人建立連結，卻覺得在公共場合互動很困難。社群媒體提供了一個舒適的替代方案。WePlay 結合遊戲與社交聊天，使用者可創建虛擬角色而不用露出生理真面目，讓害羞的使用者感到放鬆。
[Paragraph 2] WePlay works like a social playground. Users enter virtual rooms and play short party games together. Some games ask players to guess words or draw pictures. Others test logic or quick thinking. Players can also talk using voice chat while they play. Many rooms have hosts who guide the game and manage the group. The shared activity helps people start conversations naturally.
【翻譯】WePlay 就像社交遊樂場。使用者進入虛擬房間玩派對遊戲，有些猜字、畫畫或考驗邏輯敏捷思維。遊戲時還能語音聊天，共同活動幫助人們自然開啟對話。
[Paragraph 3] The app feels less like endless scrolling and more like a quick social break. A player may join a room, play a game and chat for a few minutes. A player can leave and return later. Many users say that short visits feel comfortable. For many, WePlay offers a light and playful way to meet others online.
【翻譯】這款 App 給人的感覺不像在無止境滑手機，更像簡短的社交小憩。使用者可以短暫停留、玩個遊戲聊天幾分鐘，提供輕鬆有趣的交友方式。

📌 核心文法：名詞子句當受詞[cite: 1]

句型結構： S + V + that + S + V...[cite: 1]

常用於表達「想法、事實或感受」[cite: 1]。連接詞 that 引導的名詞子句當作主要動詞（say, think, feel, find）的受詞[cite: 1]。
課文例句： Many users say that short visits feel comfortable.[cite: 1]

📌 重要片語：connect (up) with[cite: 1]

指在心理或社交層面「因為志趣相投而產生共鳴、建立連結」[cite: 1]。
例句： It is easier to connect with people with the same interests.[cite: 1]
